PETITION UNDER ARTICLE 226 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA PRAYING FOR WRIT
OF CERTIORARI AND MANDAMUS AGAINST MANIFESTLY ARBITRARY TENDER CANCELLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. That the Petitioner is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013,
   engaged in developing secure artificial intelligence infrastructure for state
   judiciary workflows.

2. That the Respondent No. 2 issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) dated 12.01.2026
   for automated case registry and tracking systems across subordinate courts.

3. That the Petitioner participated in the competitive bidding process and was
   declared the successful L-1 bidder following technical qualification.

4. That on 18.03.2026, Respondent No. 2 abruptly issued the Impugned Order cancelling
   the entire tender process without assigning reasons, opportunity of hearing, or
   recording satisfaction, directly violating the doctrine of non-arbitrariness
   enshrined under ARTICLE 14 of the Constitution of India.

GROUNDS FOR RELIEF:
A. FOR THAT the Impugned Order is vitiated by manifest arbitrariness, contrary to
   the law declared by the Supreme Court in *Shayara Bano v. Union of India (2017)*
   and *E.P. Royappa v. State of Tamil Nadu (1974)*.
B. FOR THAT the Respondent being 'State' within the meaning of ARTICLE 12 is bound
   by principles of natural justice and fairness in administrative action under
   ARTICLE 21 (Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India).
C. FOR THAT the remedy under Section 34 of Arbitration and Conciliation Act or
   statutory civil suits would cause irreparable delay, rendering Article 226 the
   sole efficacious constitutional remedy.

PRAYER:
WHEREFORE, it is respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) Issue a Writ of Certiorari or any other appropriate writ quashing the Impugned
    Order dated 18.03.2026;
(b) Issue a Writ of Mandamus directing Respondent No. 2 to finalize the contract award;
(c) Pass any further orders deemed fit in the interest of justice.
